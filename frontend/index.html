THE KOLO MUSIC MISSION

Your Music Is More Than a Song. It's an Asset.

KOLO MUSIC is built to help Liberian artists turn their music into opportunity. We are creating a platform where artists can publish their work, reach listeners, and participate in a music economy built around real value.

With KOLO SCREAM, listeners can pay for authorized access to hear a song. And with our planned download access, listeners will be able to pay for the right to download music they love. That means your music can create value beyond simply being played online.
